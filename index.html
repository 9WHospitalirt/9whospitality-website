About 9W Hospitality

A multi-engine hospitality platform — built in Punjab, scaling across India.

After 12 years building 9W into a multi-format hospitality company, we run two engines — each with its own SOPs, brand teams and infrastructure. The Society Foodcourts is our flagship: standardised, multi-brand venues anchoring high-traffic real estate from highway formats to urban premium foodcourts. Alongside it sits our brand house: 9W's own brands, Anna Paatram, and the Texas Smokehouse master franchise for Punjab.

Leadership

Two brothers. One operating discipline.

9W is run by two brothers — one with the foodcourt operating playbook honed over a decade, the other bringing Michelin-grade refinement and IHM training to the next phase of growth.

SARBESHWAR
Co-Founder & Director

Sarbeshwar Singh Chadha

The operator behind The Society Foodcourts · Operations & Growth

Sarbeshwar is the operator in the room. He co-founded 9W with his brother Sirjan in 2013, opening the first 9W Diner at Omaxe Mall, Ludhiana, and has spent the years since turning one counter into a network: six Society Foodcourts and 35+ brand counters across Punjab, with more opening.

He built the Society model on one promise to landlords and brands: 9W carries the hard part. Site selection, fit-out, staffing, central purchasing and daily reporting all run in-house, so a global QSR or a homegrown brand can open on a highway or in a city mall and trade from day one.

Today he leads the group's operations and growth: the Texas Smokehouse master franchise for Punjab, the partnerships with India's best-known QSRs, the franchise programme for Anna Paatram, and 9W's own brands, 9W Bistro, Heritage Biryani and Badmaash Kebabi, which he is now opening to franchise partners beyond 9W's own sites.

SIRJAN
Co-Founder & Director

Sirjan Singh Chadha

IHM Pusa · The Oberoi Group · Junoon NYC under Chef Vikas Khanna

Sirjan is redefining what it means to be a restaurateur in India. An alumnus of IHM Pusa, his hospitality experience spans The Oberoi Group and Michelin-starred Junoon in New York under Chef Vikas Khanna. Today he leads the next chapter of 9W Hospitality and co-founded Heritage Biryani, 9W Bistro and 9W Diner — three distinctly Indian brands with modern QSR sensibilities. Under his leadership the group has successfully launched Society Foodcourts across Kathunangal, Patiala, Bathinda, Ludhiana, Mukerian and Hoshiarpur. How We Build

Eight stages, from site to service.

Every Society Foodcourt moves through the same eight stages — from site lock to opening day. In-house teams run civil, MEP, kitchen fabrication and brand fitout in parallel, not sequentially. The same playbook, every site.

01
Locate

Greenfield + brownfield site identification, catchment study, accessibility analysis.

02
Feasibility

Catchment, population, traffic and brand-mix feasibility reports.

03
Brand Curate

Right 9W in-house and partner brands chosen for the location.

04
Proposal

Investor proposals with Minimum Guarantee + mutually agreeable lock-in.

05
Design

Floor plans — kitchens, equipment, dishwashing, service, storage, seating, washrooms, fire corridors.

06
Build

Civil, masonry, plumbing, welding, electrical, glazing, finishing — all in-house teams.

07
Launch

Plug-and-play handover. Staff onboarded, SOPs live, brands ready for cash.

08
Operate

Upkeep, maintenance, cleanliness, security and brand ops under 9W supervision.
