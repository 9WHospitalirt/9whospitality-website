Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 7 July 2026

9W Hospitality is committed to making www.9whospitality.com usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, or keyboard-only navigation.

Our target standard

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the internationally recognised standard for web accessibility. This includes:

  • A visible "Skip to main content" link on every page for keyboard users.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the mobile menu, video gallery and contact form.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Colour combinations checked for adequate contrast.
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who set that preference in their operating system.
  • Form fields with visible labels and clear error messaging.

Known limitations

We're continuing to improve the Site. If you encounter any barrier — a video without captions, an interactive element that doesn't behave well with your assistive technology, or anything else — please let us know using the contact details below, and we'll address it.

Feedback

Email info@9whospitality.com with the page URL and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
