Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026 · Applies to www.9whospitality.com and all 9W Hospitality Pvt Ltd properties referenced on it

9W Hospitality Pvt Ltd ("9W", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through www.9whospitality.com (the "Site"), why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us directly — name, email, phone number, city, and message content when you submit an inquiry through our contact form (franchise, real estate, investor, travel, press or careers).
  • Information collected automatically — pages visited, time on page, device/browser type, and approximate location, via Google Analytics 4 and similar analytics tools, once you consent via our cookie banner.
  • Social embeds — if we embed Instagram content on the Site, Meta/Instagram may set cookies or collect data per its own privacy policy when that content loads.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to and route your inquiry to the relevant team (partnerships, franchise, real estate, careers or press).
  • To send a one-time acknowledgement email confirming we've received your inquiry.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve content and performance.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your inquiry data for unrelated marketing without your separate consent (for example, our optional newsletter sign-up).

3. Cookies

The Site uses a small number of cookies: essential cookies required for the Site to function, and analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4 and, where enabled, Microsoft Clarity) that only load after you accept our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser or adjusting the banner preference.

4. Sharing your information

We share personal data only with:

  • The internal 9W team responsible for the type of inquiry you submitted.
  • Service providers who help us run the Site (hosting provider, email delivery, analytics) — bound to use your data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, where required by Indian law or a valid legal request.

5. Data retention

We retain inquiry data for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain business records, and no longer than required by applicable law. You may request earlier deletion (see Section 7).

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data submitted through the Site against unauthorised access, alteration or loss. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a data principal, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. analytics, newsletter) at any time.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@9whospitality.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

8. Children's data

The Site is intended for business and consumer inquiries from adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
